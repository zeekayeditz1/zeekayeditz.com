The approach

Build the story around what the viewer needs to know

A creator documentary often moves between different years, platforms and points of view. The edit needs to show when a clip was recorded, where a quote came from and how it connects to the next event. We work from your approved script to place source clips, screenshots, headlines and timeline graphics at the relevant moments, keeping the narration as the thread through the story.

When you request SunnyV2 style video editing, send the particular reference and timestamps that matter to you. We can discuss the balance of source footage, animated callouts, music and pauses for your subject. For related studio work, browse our cinematic documentary samples, sports stories and narrated explainers.

A timeline the viewer can follow

Dates, chapter changes and source labels connect one event to the next. Screenshots and clips stay on screen long enough for the relevant detail to register.

Graphics that point to the evidence

Document punch-ins, highlighted quotes, channel screenshots and simple comparisons make the script's evidence easier to follow. You approve the factual account and source selection.

Pacing built around the reveal

The opening establishes the central question; the middle provides context; the final section delivers the approved conclusion. Music, pauses and transitions support that structure.

Build the brief

A useful brief for a SunnyV2-style editor

Provide the final script and voiceover, source links or licensed footage, key dates and the spelling of names. Flag quotes that need attribution and material that should not be used. Include a reference video with timestamps, target runtime, deadline and the level of custom motion design you need.

Research, fact checking, scriptwriting and recording the voiceover are not assumed to be part of an editing brief; list any of those needs so availability and scope can be confirmed. ZeeKay Editz is independent. SunnyV2 is a creative reference, without an affiliation or endorsement.
