Choose by the work

A clear brief makes a better match.

If you have been comparing freelance video editors on Fiverr or Upwork, start with the samples that match your actual format. A sports documentary, cartoon commentary video and VSL need different pacing and graphics. Choose a relevant service above and compare how the edit supports the narration.

Share your channel link, a reference video, your script or footage, the finished runtime and deadline. We will confirm the graphic work, asset sourcing, raw-footage review and delivery formats in your quote. You can contact ZeeKay Editz directly or review our Fiverr seller profile.

For an ongoing channel, ask about a monthly editing partnership. For Shorts, Reels or TikTok cutdowns, include the number of versions and required aspect ratios in your brief so the work is priced clearly.
