The approach

From a player's turning point to the final whistle

A great play is only part of a sports video. A documentary needs to explain what changed, who was under pressure and why the result mattered. We arrange your narration and footage into a clear sequence, then use score graphics, player labels, replays and music to guide attention through the story.

Our portfolio below includes NBA player stories, basketball edits and an Erling Haaland football video. Use these samples to judge the pacing and visual treatment before hiring. Whether you need a long-form sports documentary editor or a shorter highlight edit, send the final runtime, footage plan and a reference so we can quote the right scope.

Sports documentary editing

Player histories, rivalry stories and turning points shaped around your script, with transitions between the setup, action and outcome.

Highlights with context

Action selections, replay timing and clear player identification that help the viewer understand the moment without losing momentum.

Graphics and sound

On-screen stats, names and supporting motion graphics, with music changes and sound design suited to the story's energy.
