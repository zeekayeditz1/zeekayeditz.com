The approach

A true crime edit needs a clear chain of events

The viewer needs to know who is speaking, what has happened and why a detail matters. A true crime editor can bring that context into the picture with dates, location labels, document punch-ins, maps and a sequence that follows the script's timeline. Music and pauses can support the narration without turning every moment into a reveal.

Bodycam footage adds a different challenge: lengthy recordings, changing audio levels and details that may be easy to miss. A bodycam video editor selects the relevant moments, uses captions where speech needs support and places the footage inside your approved commentary. For a practical example of building an opening, see our bodycam editing and After Effects hooks guide.

Evidence in context

Photos, approved documents, dates and location graphics appear when the narration discusses them. We distinguish the footage, the speaker's commentary and any labels supplied in your brief.

Watchable footage breakdowns

Long recordings are organized into the moments needed for the story. Dialogue editing, volume balancing, selected captions and agreed timestamps help the viewer follow the sequence.

Sensitive details handled to scope

You can specify faces, names, plates or other details that need blurring, muting or removal. The source review, redactions and platform delivery requirements are agreed before editing.

Build the brief

What to send your true crime or bodycam editor

Share the approved script, narration, case outline and source recordings. Include source timestamps, names and spellings, the planned runtime and any details that must be redacted. For several hours of raw footage, say whether you have already selected the story moments or need footage review included.

You approve the factual account and the rights to source material. We agree research, asset sourcing, blurring and delivery versions in the quote. If you want a creator-documentary approach with timelines and online sources, also explore our SunnyV2-style service.

Before you start

True crime and bodycam questions

Can I hire a true crime video editor for a recurring channel?

Yes. Send the channel, a completed episode, your next script and upload schedule. We can agree a repeatable visual style for intros, evidence graphics, captions and full episodes.

Do you offer bodycam video editing?

Yes. Bodycam editing can include footage selection, commentary assembly, dialogue cleanup, captions, timestamps and agreed redactions. The volume of raw footage and review needed is part of the scope.

Can you make true crime edits from my script and voiceover?

Yes. Share the narration, script and agreed source assets. We build the visual sequence around the story and confirm whether extra research, stock footage or motion graphics are included.
