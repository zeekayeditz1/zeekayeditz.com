The approach

An edit that lets viewers follow the game

The best reaction means little if the viewer missed the setup. We shape gaming footage so the objective, mistake or unexpected moment is understandable before the payoff. That means removing dead time, keeping useful voice chat, choosing when to show facecam and leaving space for the reaction itself.

For narrated gaming guides and explainers, the screen needs to show what the voiceover describes. Our GTA examples below demonstrate that format, while the Arc Raiders sample shows a gameplay-focused edit. Share the game, audience and editing references so we can agree how much captioning, meme editing and motion graphics your video needs.

Gameplay and highlight structure

A clear opening, useful context and selected moments, with quiet sections trimmed around the story you want to tell.

Facecam, voice chat and captions

Reactions synchronized with the gameplay, readable captions where they help, and audio balance between the game and commentary.

Narrated guides

Voiceover-matched gameplay, step labels, zooms and callouts so viewers can understand the action or follow a tutorial.

Your project brief

What to send for a gaming edit

Share your gameplay and facecam files, separate microphone audio if available, a short outline or timestamps of key moments, and a reference video. Tell us how many hours of raw footage there are, the final runtime and whether you also need vertical cutdowns. Footage review time and extra versions are included in the agreed scope.

Before you hire

Gaming videos editing questions

Can you edit funny gaming moments and meme videos?

Yes. Send the gameplay, voice chat or facecam and a reference for the humor and pacing you want. We can agree the captions, cutaways and sound effects in your sample edit.

Do you edit gaming guides as well as gameplay?

Yes. Our portfolio includes GTA explainers and an Arc Raiders gameplay sample. Guides can include voiceover-matched footage, step labels, captions and supporting graphics.

Can you make Shorts from a longer gaming video?

Yes, vertical cutdowns can be scoped with the main edit. Tell us how many clips you need and whether they require reframing, captions or a separate opening hook.

How much does a gaming editor cost?

Growth editing starts at $60 per video up to 10 minutes at the volume rate. Signature After Effects editing is $400 for a 15-minute video. The amount of raw footage, graphic work and additional versions affect the quote.
