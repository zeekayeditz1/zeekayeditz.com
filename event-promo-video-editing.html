The approach

Event video editing with a clear message and date

An event promo has to communicate both the experience and the next step. We shape the opening around the strongest supplied material, build a sequence that introduces the activities or speakers, and make the date, venue and registration details readable. Music and transitions support the intended energy while the important information stays clear.

Event 01 is the sample from the Event Promo section on our homepage. Use it to discuss the structure and graphic treatment for your announcement. If you are promoting a freelance service rather than a dated event, explore gig and seller promo editing. For a longer offer explanation, compare video sales letter editing.

A sequence built from your event assets

Supplied venue footage, previous-event clips, speaker images or activity shots are selected around the campaign message. Tell us whether the promo is an announcement, reminder or final call.

Readable event information

Dates, times, venue names, speaker details and booking information follow your approved copy. End cards and graphic emphasis make the intended action easy to identify.

Versions for the campaign

A main promo and agreed shorter versions can be prepared together. Each runtime, aspect ratio, caption treatment and end card is listed in the scope before editing.

Build the brief

What to send your event promo editor

Provide the event brief, date and time, venue or online destination, registration link, logo and approved wording. Include footage, photographs, speaker assets and any music or brand guidelines. List the final runtimes, formats, deadline and planned release dates.

If the campaign needs updates as speakers or event details change, tell us which versions are planned. This service covers the edit and agreed graphic work from supplied or approved assets. Filming, asset sourcing and additional design work need to be included explicitly in the quote.

Before you start

Event promo videos questions

Can you edit a promotional video for my event?

Yes. Send the campaign message, approved event information and source assets. We can build an announcement or teaser with the agreed pacing, graphics and call to action.

Can you use photos and previous-event footage?

Yes. Share the available assets so we can assess how they fit the sequence. We agree any additional stock sourcing or design work before production.

Can you create different event teaser versions?

Yes. List the platforms, lengths, aspect ratios and any wording changes. A clear version list lets us include the full campaign delivery in the quote.

What affects event promo editing cost?

The amount of source material, graphics, finished runtime, number of versions and turnaround requirements affect the scope. Send the assets and campaign brief for a quote.
