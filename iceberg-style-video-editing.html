The approach

What is the iceberg video format?

The iceberg video format organizes a subject into layers. The top usually introduces familiar entries; deeper tiers move into lesser-known ideas, stories or theories. The chart is a navigation device, helping viewers understand where an entry sits and what comes next. Your script should establish which entries are factual, disputed or speculative before the edit begins.

An iceberg video editor turns that structure into an episode: introduce the chart, move to the current tier, explain an entry, then return to the structure before the next descent. The visuals may include source clips, screenshots, diagrams and labels. For a narrated piece without tiers, compare YouTube explainer editing.

A chart that stays readable

We can design an iceberg chart with your supplied tiers and entries, then animate tier changes and camera moves. Entry names remain readable before attention shifts to the explanation.

Chapter and entry continuity

Repeatable title cards, color choices and transitions make long videos easier to follow. We agree how often to revisit the chart and how to separate entries without stopping the narration.

Atmosphere with a clear voice

Music, ambience and color can change as the episode moves deeper. The narration remains the reference for pacing and audio balance, including longer explanations that need more space.

Build the brief

Plan the chart before the edit

Send your topic, tier list, approved script and voiceover, with sources or assets for each entry. Include the final runtime, reference videos and any content that needs particular care. If the chart already exists, share an editable version; otherwise tell us how many tiers and entries it needs.

We agree the amount of chart animation, source review and custom graphics before quoting. For a series, a shared set of entry cards, typography and chart treatments can carry across episodes while the content changes.

Before you start

Iceberg explained videos questions

Can you edit an iceberg explained video from my narration?

Yes. Send the script, voiceover, tier list and agreed source assets. We assemble the episode around your entry order and build the agreed chart and chapter treatments.

Does iceberg video editing include an animated chart?

It can. Share an existing chart or specify the tiers and entries needed. Custom chart design and animation are confirmed as part of your quote.

How is an iceberg video different from a normal explainer?

An iceberg video uses a visible tier structure to organize entries and guide the viewer deeper into a topic. A general explainer can follow a timeline, argument or process without that chart.
