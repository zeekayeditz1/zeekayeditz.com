Iceberg Style Video Editing

Tier reveals, chart camera pushes and an atmosphere that darkens with every layer — the format built for deep-dive retention.

What makes an iceberg video binge-worthy

Iceberg videos live or die on descent. Every layer needs to feel deeper than the last: camera pushes into the chart that sell the drop, entry title cards with consistent motion language, color and light that darken per tier — bright surface, murky depths — and a score that thickens as the entries get stranger. The chart itself is a character: we design and animate it in After Effects with custom tiers, entry thumbnails and submerged lighting.

Between reveals, the editing supports long-form narration: b-roll and archival punch-ins timed to key facts, captions for names and dates, and chapter pacing that keeps viewers committed through hour-long runtimes. That structure is why the format retains so well when it is cut properly.
