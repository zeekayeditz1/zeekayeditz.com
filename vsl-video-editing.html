VSL Video Editing

Video sales letters engineered to convert — 5-second hooks, dynamic captions, pattern interrupts and CTA graphics that turn viewers into buyers.

What makes a VSL edit actually convert

A VSL is not a YouTube video — every editing decision has one job: keep the prospect watching until the offer. That means a hook engineered for the first 5 seconds, dynamic captions because most viewers start muted, b-roll that visually proves each claim the moment it is spoken, and a pattern interrupt every 20–30 seconds — zoom shifts, graphic pops, angle changes — that resets attention before it drifts.

We also handle the conversion furniture: testimonial cutaways with name plates, animated stat callouts, urgency banners and CTA end cards that match your funnel. Send your raw talking-head footage and script, and get back a VSL that is structured, captioned and ready to run traffic to.
