The approach

What does a VSL editor do?

A VSL editor turns your approved script and recordings into a video sales letter. The work includes selecting takes, removing distractions, placing relevant B-roll, building captions and graphics, and balancing the voice, music and sound effects. Each visual should support what the speaker is explaining, whether that is a problem, a product demonstration or the offer itself.

We build the edit around your audience and where the video will appear: a sales page, webinar funnel or paid campaign. A longer explanation may need a calm demonstration; a short version may need a tighter opening. For the terms and planning process, see our VSL editing guide. For production decisions, compare the two studio samples below.

An opening with a clear promise

We tighten the introduction around your approved message, choose the strongest take and bring in visual context early. Hook alternatives can be quoted if you want to test different openings.

Proof that viewers can read

Product screens, demonstrations, supplied testimonials and approved statistics appear beside the relevant claim. Captions, name plates and callouts stay legible at the intended viewing size.

A coherent offer and next step

The edit makes the product, included benefits and call to action easy to follow. We can prepare a master video and agreed shorter versions, with consistent fonts, color and audio.

Build the brief

What to send for a VSL video editing quote

Send the approved script, raw footage or voiceover, product link, brand assets and examples of the visual direction you want. Include the intended audience, placement, finished runtime, aspect ratios and deadline. If you need hook variants, captions in separate files or several calls to action, list each version so it can be included in the scope.

You supply or approve the sales claims, testimonials, music and source assets. We agree whether stock sourcing, screen recordings, script changes or custom animation are included before production. The editing quote is separate from traffic buying and campaign management.

Before you start

VSL editing questions

What is VSL editing?

VSL editing means editing a video sales letter: a video that explains an offer and asks the viewer to take an action. It combines the script, voice or footage, demonstrations, captions, proof and call to action into one clear sequence.

Can I hire a VSL editor for my existing footage?

Yes. Send your recorded material, script and brand assets. We can assemble a new video sales letter or revise an existing VSL edit, with the amount of footage review and rework agreed in the quote.

Do you create VSL edits and shorter ad versions?

Yes. A master video, alternative openings and shorter versions can be planned together. Each required runtime, aspect ratio and variation should be listed in your brief.

How much does a VSL video editor cost?

Growth editing starts at $60 per video up to 10 minutes at the volume rate. Signature After Effects editing is $400 for a 15-minute video. Footage review, complex animation and extra versions affect the final scope.

Can a VSL edit guarantee conversions?

No. Editing can improve clarity and presentation, but campaign results also depend on the offer, audience, traffic and landing page. We agree the creative deliverables and use your campaign feedback to guide any planned revisions.
