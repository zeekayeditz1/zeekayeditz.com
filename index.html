About Us

Crafting Cinematic Stories Through Motion

We're ZeeKay Editz, a creative video editing and motion design agency founded by Zahid Ali. We specialize in transforming raw footage into visually stunning content — from cinematic video edits and motion graphics to custom After Effects animations and full-scale YouTube productions.

With a deep passion for the craft and a keen eye for detail, our team works closely with content creators, brands, and agencies to bring creative visions to life. A remote-first team of 8 editors working across time zones — trusted by YouTube creators and brands in the US, UK, Europe, and beyond.
