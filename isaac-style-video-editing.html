We use those references to agree the density of visuals, the amount of on-screen text, movement and sound your own script needs.

Before you start

Isaac-style editing questions

Can I hire an Isaac style video editor?

Yes. Send your script or footage and point to the parts of Isaac 01 that best describe your direction. We can use a sample edit to agree the treatment for your channel.

Which Isaac-style reference do you use?

The Isaac 01 portfolio video linked on this page is the reference for the service. If you mean another video or a particular part of that style, include its URL and timestamps in the brief.

Can the edit use my fonts and branding?

Yes. Share the brand assets and any recurring elements from your channel. We can adapt the agreed pacing and graphic direction around that identity.

What affects the editing quote?

Finished runtime, footage review, graphic complexity, asset sourcing and delivery versions affect the scope. Share a script excerpt and reference before choosing the production treatment.
