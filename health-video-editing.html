The approach

Health and wellness video editing starts with a clear script

A health explainer often asks the viewer to follow unfamiliar words, comparisons or a sequence of steps. The edit can make that easier through a relevant demonstration, a simple label or a comparison that remains on screen long enough to read. We match the visuals to your approved narration and keep names, measurements and terminology consistent with the material you provide.

The two Health samples below are the videos from our homepage health section. This service suits educational health, food and wellness channels using a prepared script and voiceover. For a broader narrated format, explore YouTube explainer editing; for a sketch-based presentation, compare hand drawn explainer editing.

Visuals matched to the explanation

Supplied footage, approved stock, screenshots and diagrams appear beside the point in the narration they support. Asset sourcing is agreed before the edit starts.

Readable terms and comparisons

On-screen labels, captions and comparison graphics help viewers follow the script. You provide the approved spelling, figures and sources for any information shown.

A consistent educational style

Typography, chapter cards, music and transitions can be kept consistent across a recurring series. The amount of movement and emphasis follows your audience and reference.

Build the brief

What to send for a health video edit

Provide the approved script, recorded narration, channel reference and brand assets. Include terminology, source links and the exact wording or numbers required in graphics. Tell us the target audience, finished runtime, aspect ratios and deadline.

The editing brief defines the presentation work. Your subject expert or content owner approves the health information and any product statements. Let us know whether you are supplying footage and diagrams or need asset sourcing and custom motion graphics quoted.

Before you start

Health and wellness videos questions

Do you edit health and nutrition videos for YouTube?

Yes. We edit prepared health, wellness and nutrition content using your approved script, narration and agreed source assets. The samples above show existing studio work in this category.

Can you add diagrams, labels and captions?

Yes. Supporting graphics can include terminology labels, comparison layouts, chapter cards and captions. More detailed diagrams or animation are agreed in the scope.

Does the edit include writing or reviewing health claims?

The service starts from your approved content. Send material that has been reviewed by your subject expert, including the wording, figures and sources to show on screen.

What affects health video editing pricing?

Finished runtime, source review, asset sourcing, captions, diagrams and delivery versions affect the quote. Share a reference and a sample of your script so we can agree the right treatment.
