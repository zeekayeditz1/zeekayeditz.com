The approach

Turn a narration into a visual explanation

An explainer needs a visual reason for every cut. A comparison may need two images side by side; a sequence needs a timeline; a difficult idea may need a simple graphic and a longer pause. We work from your narration to choose the treatment that makes each point easier to follow.

This service fits voiceover-led channels, gaming explainers and commentary essays. It can combine supplied footage, screenshots, character cutouts, text and After Effects motion graphics. Our hand-drawn-style sample and GTA explainers below show different visual approaches. For a tier-based mystery format, see iceberg video editing; for cinematic storytelling, see Ryan Pictures style editing.

Narration-to-visual structure

Footage and imagery placed around the claims, examples and transitions in your script, rather than a stream of unrelated B-roll.

Motion graphics that explain

Labels, diagrams, timelines, comparisons and typography designed to support the point your viewer is hearing.

A repeatable channel style

A consistent approach to chapters, colors, captions and sound so recurring uploads feel like part of the same channel.

Before you hire

Explainers & video essays editing questions

Do you edit faceless YouTube videos?

Yes. We can build a narrated edit from your script, voiceover and agreed footage or assets. Source research, stock assets and custom animation are confirmed in the scope.

Can you add diagrams and After Effects motion graphics?

Yes. Supporting labels, timelines, comparisons and animated text can be included. More detailed motion design is quoted as part of a Signature edit or a custom scope.

Does this include writing the script or recording the voiceover?

The editing service starts from your script and voiceover. If you need other production work, mention it in the brief so we can confirm availability and price before you commit.

What does explainer video editing cost?

Growth editing starts at $60 per video up to 10 minutes at the volume rate. Signature After Effects editing is $400 for a 15-minute video. Custom animation, research and asset sourcing are agreed separately in the quote.
